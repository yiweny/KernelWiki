## Summary

Two B300 (CC 10.3) fixes found while benchmarking DeepSeek-V3 MLA decode shapes:

1. **Backend table gap**: the `"10.3"` row of `routine_cc_to_supported_backends` omitted `cute-dsl` and `auto` even though the cute-dsl MLA path only requires SM100+ and is the **fastest** backend on the measured shapes. Benchmarks silently skipped the winner.
2. **Cluster-aware split-KV**: `set_split_kv` budgeted splits as `sm_count / B`, but each split is a full CTA **cluster** (`ClusterShape[0] = 2` for the 2-SM kernel). Over-splitting ~2× wastes waves and doubles LSE-reduction work.

## Kernel Relevance

SM103 bring-up must (a) treat cute-dsl MLA as first-class on 10.3, and (b) schedule split-KV in **cluster slots**, not raw SM count, for 2-SM Blackwell MLA.
